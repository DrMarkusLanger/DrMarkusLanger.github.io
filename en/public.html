Public Engagement and Transfer

I speak and write above all about human oversight and control of AI, about psychology and AI, and about the societal effects of AI — in keynotes, in media interviews and in other formats of science communication. Concretely, this often concerns AI in healthcare and psychotherapy, trust in AI, and AI and psychological implications for the future of work.

In addition, I advise on the good introduction of AI in organisations and in public administration, and on questions of AI governance.

This page shows a selection of my media contributions, keynote talks and work at the interface with policy. Most below media contributions are in German.
